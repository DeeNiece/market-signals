How the signals work

MA cross: the simple 9-period average closes above the simple 26-period average, and is still above it at the latest close.

HMA turn: the 26-period Hull MA stops falling and starts rising, and is still rising at the latest close. No minimum size is applied, so a turn on a flat line can be weak.

Price vs HMA 26: whether the latest close is above the Hull MA. 9 vs 26 SMA: the gap between the two averages.

Earnings: shown only when results are due within 7 days of the scan date, because signals around earnings are less reliable.

Averages are calculated from TradingView daily closes. TradingView's own indicators may differ slightly. This page is a signal list for reference, not investment advice.
